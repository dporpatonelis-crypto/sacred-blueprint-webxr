# Γαλιλαία

## Πανόραμα 360° · ImageGen

```text
Create a seamless 2:1 equirectangular 360-degree painted environment map for an educational WebXR world on the ancient shore of the Sea of Galilee near Tiberias at dawn. Full spherical panorama with one continuous level natural horizon near the horizontal equator. Open blue freshwater lake occupies the forward half of the panorama, reflecting a soft line of golden morning light, with layered blue-grey Galilean hills on the opposite shore. The view turns naturally into a shore of pale limestone and dark basalt pebbles, small olive groves and sloping grassy fields. Understated reeds at the waterline, distant hills, warm ivory haze, cool lapis and cyan-blue water, gentle amber dawn, patches of olive green. Match the contemporary neo-Byzantine biblical narrative paintings in a Greek educational video: visibly fine hand-painted egg-tempera and fresco textures, muted mineral pigments, warm dignified light and convincing spatial depth. Keep the lower quarter spacious and subdued so a walkable 3D beach, rocks, nets and a boat can occupy the foreground. The far-left and far-right edges show the same continuous quiet hillside and sky at matching height and color, suitable for a seamless wrap. Sky and ground extend to their poles. Scenery only, no people, close-up objects, boats, lettering, buildings from a modern era, interface, frames, deliberate seams or collage. One coherent illuminated landscape.
```

Το πανόραμα δημιουργήθηκε με το ενσωματωμένο ImageGen και εξήχθη σε WebP. Η βάρκα, τα δίχτυα και η όχθη είναι τρισδιάστατα αντικείμενα στη σκηνή.
