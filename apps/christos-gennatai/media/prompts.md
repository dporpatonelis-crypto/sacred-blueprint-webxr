# Εικαστικά Βηθλεέμ

Οι δύο νέες εικόνες δημιουργήθηκαν με το ενσωματωμένο ImageGen. Τα αρχικά PNG φυλάσσονται στον ίδιο φάκελο και οι εκδόσεις WebP φορτώνονται στην εφαρμογή. Η υφή 360° έχει ομαλή μετάβαση στα άκρα και η εικόνα εισόδου έχει διαστάσεις 1600 × 900 για τις έξι υποδοχές.

## Πανόραμα 360°

```text
Create a new 360-degree equirectangular skybox panorama for a stylized educational WebXR lesson, a quiet night around ancient Bethlehem at the Nativity. Exact 2:1 equirectangular world panorama, full spherical wrap with a level horizon near the middle, natural sky at the top and simple distant earth at the bottom. The extreme left and right edges meet seamlessly in dark blue distant hills and a quiet continuous indigo sky, with the same brightness and few details at the join. Place the visual focus near the middle of the panorama: a single bright golden Star of Bethlehem, clear and poised over a distant cluster of warm limestone houses and an illuminated cave in a gentle hillside. Judean ridges, a few distant olive trees, subtle points of lantern light, dry historical terrain. Expanse of clear deep cobalt and ultramarine, rich ochre stone, soft gold flecks, fine painted textures and restrained illumination inspired by illuminated manuscript miniatures and naturalistic Byzantine Nativity art. Keep all architecture in the mid- or far distance, leave the near ground visually quiet so actual Three.js platforms and props can sit in front. A luminous, quiet, human atmosphere with readable tonal separation. No lettering, border, modern buildings, Christmas ornaments, snow, duplicated star, interface, characters, frames or visible seams. One cohesive painted panorama.
```

## Εικόνα εισόδου

```text
Generate a new single continuous widescreen 16:9 illustration for a six-piece image puzzle in a Greek educational lesson about the Nativity: the nocturnal path to Bethlehem. Authentic ancient Judean stone steps lead through olive groves from the lower foreground to the warm golden opening of a stone cave, with a single Star of Bethlehem above and terraced limestone houses in the distance. A deep cobalt blue and ultramarine sky, gold star and warm lamp light, sand and ochre stone, an olive tree, rich fine-grained painterly texture in the spirit of an illuminated medieval manuscript miniature, naturalistic composition and warm respectful mood. This will be invisibly cut into a regular 3-column by 2-row grid: make each of the six areas visually recognizable, using unique continuous contours and complementary details crossing the boundaries; avoid blank patches of uniform sky. Arrange distant hillside houses toward the upper-left, the gold star and its soft shaft around upper-middle, a distinctive dark olive crown on the upper-right, limestone step line and terracotta lamp in the lower-left, glowing cave entrance in the lower-middle, olive roots and golden rocks on the lower-right. Tie them into a single carefully balanced scene without explicit sections. No drawn grid, no jigsaw outlines, no lettering, no numbers, no border, no panels, no modern lights, no snow, no additional starburst. Artwork only.
```

## Υλικό του αρχικού μαθήματος

Τα κείμενα και οι μάσκες προέρχονται από το τοπικό επεξεργάσιμο έργο, του οποίου το HTML ταυτίζεται με το αντικείμενο Git `1ef407335208fa84cf2c75adbc9be24ff0d5d636` του [Digital Library](https://dporpatonelis-crypto.github.io/digital-library-core/lessons/christos-gennatai.html). Η παρτιτούρα και το SHA-256 της ψηφιακής απόδοσης καταγράφονται στο manifest ήχου.
