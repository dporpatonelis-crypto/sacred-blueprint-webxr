# Πανόραμα και πλανητικά φόντα

Παραγωγή με το ενσωματωμένο ImageGen. Οι αρχικές εικόνες των μαθημάτων προέρχονται από το HTML της Ψηφιακής Βιβλιοθήκης.

## hub

Create an environment texture for a Greek educational Three.js WebXR collection, a wide 2:1 equirectangular 360-degree environment panorama from a stationary observer, seam at the two ends matched in value, color and forms, level equator at mid-height and simple top and bottom poles. A gently stylized illustrated planetary observatory sky, like a warm painterly 3D storybook landscape seen beyond low-poly cream limestone platforms and turquoise water. Paint only the distant space environment itself: luminous deep petroleum-blue and softened teal depth, quiet small stars, distant restrained bands of golden dust, a broad aqua atmospheric glow and softly lit rock silhouettes low at a distant stable horizon. The observer will see five real 3D planet models, golden orbital paths, lesson illustrations and parchment in front of this texture, so leave clear low-contrast space for them at eye height. Strong but soft horizon detail, elegant material and brushwork, subtle warm reflected brass light and no monochromatic black void. Mediterranean storybook warmth carried into a planetary setting, visually rich enough for the central hub yet legible. Avoid giant foreground planets, paths, structures, figures, interface, typography, symbols, borders or watermarks. Preserve a believable continuous circular projection. Landscape texture, intended at 2:1.

## judaism

Environment texture for a spatial educational WebXR lesson in Greek, coherent with the Sacred Blueprint collection's serene painterly, stylized 3D landscapes with warm limestone platforms, parchment and turquoise water. Generate one new wide 2:1 equirectangular 360-degree panorama for a quiet individual planet study area, with faint ochre and pale limestone folds beyond the horizon, quiet warm amber dust and low blue-green evening air. Distant atmosphere only, simple high sky with restrained flecks of starlight, horizon exactly at mid-height, both joined edges matching in tone and silhouetted forms, level orientation and simple poles. Deep teal values at eye height keep clear silhouettes of real foreground glass vessels, circular art and parchment that will be rendered in 3D. Painterly tonal materials, subtle depth, deliberate negative space, readable warm light. Calm, softly lit cosmic landscape in the manner of a Mediterranean illustrated storybook. No characters, religious iconography, text, interfaces, paths, platforms, props, giant planets, borders or watermarks. No center focal object. Intended output 2:1 panoramic environment texture.
