# Ιορδάνης

## ImageGen · Πανόραμα 360°

```text
Create a new 360-degree equirectangular skybox for an educational Three.js journey along the ancient River Jordan. Exact 2:1 panoramic aspect, full spherical horizontal wrap, both far edges meet in the same quiet pale limestone ridge and a continuous softly painted sky, level horizon just below the midpoint. Early golden morning in the first-century Judean wilderness. A shallow winding turquoise-green Jordan passes between pale stone, tan gravel, olive scrub and narrow reed beds in the middle distance. Beyond it are low layered desert hills, honey-coloured cliffs and a few fine palms far away. Gentle gold on the water, muted lapis and sage shadows, airy cream-blue sky, soft dry atmosphere. Design like a contemporary neo-Byzantine educational narrative painting with fine egg-tempera and fresco textures, plausible daylight and spacious pictorial depth. Make the lower quarter calm and sparse so real 3D water, rocks and a landing can occupy the foreground. Keep most reeds small and off centre, no heavy silhouettes at the seam. This is scenery only, no people, boats, arches, lettering, UI, frames, landmarks, visible seam or collage. One coherent, luminous and quiet painting.
```
